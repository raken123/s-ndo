BODY PARTY

Your body is the controller. Up to 6 players at once!

📺 Having a party? Connect this screen to a TV and put the camera under or on top of it.
🧍 Stand 2–3 m back so the camera can see your whole body.

How do I control it?
  • 👋 Swipe your arm left or right to switch games.
  • 🙌 Raise both hands above your head to start.
  • ✋ Hover your hand over a button to press it.
  • 🙅 Cross your arms to leave a game.
  • 🐸 Frog-jump 5 times (squat down, then jump) to open Settings.
  • ⌨️ In Settings you can submit your own game idea by typing with your hands.
