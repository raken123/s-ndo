The free meter

100% is about 20 minutes on Flash Live, or about 8 on Extended Thinking (2.5× usage).

When it hits 0%

It refills to 100% after 1 hour (30 minutes on Pro). With credits you keep talking meanwhile; without them your character gets knocked out until the refill.

Playshow costs the same

One second of episode audio uses one second of the meter, or one credit. A short skit uses about 5%.

Install in a minute

The apps aren't signed by a paid developer account yet, so your computer asks once before opening them.

Windows

  1. Run the .exe installer.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open BFDI Talk from the Start menu.

macOS (Apple Silicon)

  1. Open the .dmg and drag BFDI Talk into Applications.
  2. Open Terminal and run:
    xattr -cr "/Applications/BFDI Talk.app"
  3. Open BFDI Talk and allow the microphone.

Android

  1. Download the .apk on your phone and tap it.
  2. Allow "install unknown apps" if Android asks.
  3. Open BFDI Talk and allow the microphone (and the camera for video live).

Questions

Is this an official BFDI app?

No. BFDI Talk is an unofficial fan app. Battle for Dream Island belongs to jacknjellify. The characters you make are your own original objects.

Do I need an account?

No. You can talk and even switch plans as a guest; your credits are kept on that device. An account keeps your credits and plan on every device.

How do I pay?

You don't, right now. Lite and Pro are free: tap a plan and you're on it. BFDI Talk never asks for a card.

What's different when I play it on this page?

This page runs inside Claude, which can't use your microphone or reach Gemini. So here you type, Claude writes your character's replies, and your browser reads them out loud while the mouth moves. Playshow episodes are written by Claude too. The full app talks out loud with Gemini Live, sees you on video and walks out of the window in Agent Mode.

Where do my voice and video go?

During a call, your voice (and your camera or screen if you turn them on) is sent to Google's Gemini AI so your character can understand and react. BFDI Talk doesn't save your conversations.

What can Agent Mode do on my computer?

Timers, a to-do list, notes, copying text, opening websites or a search you ask for, looking things up, and telling the time. With Pro it can look at your screen when you ask. It can't click or type in other apps.

Is it kid-friendly?

Characters are told to stay kind and kid-friendly. Like any AI it can make mistakes, so younger fans should chat with a grown-up nearby, and a parent should handle any payments.
