Controls
  • VR/MR: point with a controller or your hand, trigger / pinch to select. Grip to grab the book, mat and tasbih.
  • Desktop: drag to look, W A S D to move, click to select, Esc for the menu.
  • Wrist menu: look at your left wrist (or press M) to open the hub.

Location is used only on this device to compute Qibla direction and prayer times. Nothing is uploaded. Qur'an text and recitation beyond the built-in selection are fetched from public Qur'an APIs when you are online.
